# AILEEN: Step outline (private)

Conventions: [W] = writer's scene from V10. [P] = proposal. Value charge in brackets, e.g. [+ -> -].
Word budget for the treatment (about 6,000 words): 8 sequences, 700-800 each.

## Sequence I: THE YEAR (Tsnori)
Q: Will James keep the village alive without Elysium? Turn: Mira volunteers, greenhouses burn.
1. Prologue: Lukas in the Chamber; Cy says she won't come back. [W] [+ -> -]
2. Mira awake, writes the page in the dark. [W]
3. Kitchen: tags, porridge halved, ten jars, "One more year." Mira gives the Tally Book. The unused box, the horse. [W]
4. Classroom: Homo sapiens lesson; Nino with the horse. [W]
5. Greenhouse: "They grow anyway." Bell. [W]
6. Square: Anton, Giorgi's argument, the lottery. Nino named. James offers a year; Anton's "two numbers" speech. [W]
7. Anton listens to a voice only he hears from a small resonator pendant (Lukas), then says "Not you." [P] Plants Lukas as the hand behind Anton.
8. Mira volunteers; "Read it"; James hits Anton; Anton cancels deliveries. [W] [+ -> --]
9. Four grey men burn the finished houses; eighty people watch. [W]
Opponent: Lukas, through Anton, burns the village's future. First hostile act lands in I.

## Sequence II: THE EMPTY CUPBOARD
Q: Can James buy back a winter? Turn: he decides to cross.
10. Church: eight children, the horse on the table. [W]
11. Weeds in burnt glass; BEANS tag; James finds the folded page. [W]
12. Night bell; white point over the ridge. [W] Second light = scale. [P: second light on another ridge appears in Seq III.]
13. Giorgi's confession: Mira was with child. [W]
14. James reads the page in front of the empty bed. [P: he reads the whole page; we do not hear it yet.]
15. Pre-dawn departure; the mountain road; the swing. [W]
Opponent: the embargo itself is Lukas's pressure; the village starves.

## Sequence III: THE GATE
Q: Will James give his yes, and to whom? Turn: he crosses.
16. Reservoir, no towers: the warehouses, the dam. [W]
17. Gate screen already knows his name. "I've come to take my wife's place." [W]
18. Maria receives him: calm, kind, knows Mira. She is not hostile and not on his side. [P]
19. Lukas on a screen, now a man of 60s, courteous. Mira is gone with a newborn who can open the Anchor. He offers the supplies forever if James finds them. [P]
20. James reads the page again. Last line: "Don't bring us back. Open it." He does not obey. [P] HINGE.
21. James consents in the Anchoring Chamber: the only way in. [P] HINGE: we watch him choose.
Opponent: Lukas makes the deal, sets Anton to shadow James. He needs the yes; he is patient and sincere.

## Sequence IV: THE DRIFT
Q: Can James read Mira's trail before Anton does? Turn: Anton needs James.
22. James arrives in the Drift: a drowned city that rearranges. [P]
23. Anton already there, tracking dead. Sandro with him. [P]
24. First tag: Mira's calligraphy on a gutter sign. James reads it. Anton cannot. [P]
25. First tide: the city turns; they are separated. [P]
26. James lays a false trail. Anton follows the wrong one. [P] James is active.
Opponent: Lukas seals the Drift's exits from his side and sends his men in.

## Sequence V: THE WARD
Q: Can the two men survive the Drift together? Turn: alliance.
27. A copy of a hospital ward. The Holdouts, first sight: an old farmer, a child. [P]
28. Tide traps both in a stairwell; James saves Anton or Anton saves James. [P]
29. Night talk: Anton's eleven years, "Not one of you asked my name." James asks. [W echo]
30. The farmer shows them a door to Mira. [P]
31. Maria's message arrives: she is a Holdout ally. [P]
Opponent: Lukas speaks to Anton again; Anton lies to James about what he said.

## Sequence VI: MIRA (midpoint)
Q: Will James bring them home? Turn: Anton's orders.
32. Mira, serene, half merged. Reunion, anger, calm. [P]
33. Aileen, already a toddler. James is a father. [P] HINGE.
34. Mira tells him what the page meant and why she left. Love: the child can only live where the door can be opened. [P]
35. Anton steps in: orders to stop Aileen. [P] Midpoint turn on James's own side: his ally is the danger.
36. Lukas's voice through Anton's pendant: the offer, the first time we see him lie. [P]
Opponent: Lukas makes his move through Anton.

## Sequence VII: THE TIDE
Q: Can James keep Aileen? Turn: Mira goes.
37. Anton takes Aileen; Holdouts fight; the farmer dies. [P]
38. James and Anton fight; Mira walks into the Ocean to stop them. [P] HINGE: James lets her go.
39. James runs with Aileen. Anton hesitates, does not shoot. [P]
40. The Drift begins to close; Aileen's clock (day 39). [P]
41. Maria cut: she is stopped at the Anchor, buys them minutes. [P] Guide removed by end of 2b.
Opponent: Lukas pursues with everything; Anton is still his man, barely.

## Sequence VIII: THE DOOR
Q: Open or close? Turn: the sacrifice.
42. The Anchor core: Lukas present, bounded, holding Sissi. [P]
43. Lukas's offer: close the door, Mira comes back, held. [P] HINGE.
44. James reads the last line of the page aloud. Chooses the open door. [P] HINGE.
45. Aileen opens the Anchor. James stands in the threshold; his free yes holds it. [P]
46. Anton stands between James and the guards. Chooses. [P] HINGE.
47. Lukas steps through with Sissi. [P]
48. James's body dies in the compound. [P]
49. Tsnori: greenhouses rebuilt. Giorgi and Nino hear the resonator. "Hey." [W/P]

## Checks
- **Mentor/ally removed:** Maria cut in VII; the farmer dies in VII; Mira gone in VII.
- **Midpoint on James's side:** Anton, his partner, is the threat.
- **Earned means:** the free yes was given in III and tested in VIII.
- **Protagonist forces climax:** James brings Aileen to the Anchor.
- **Cost:** his body, Mira, his return in person.
- **Discover, don't disclose:** the page's last line is read by James in III and acted on in VIII.
- **Open placeholders:** how Mira found the Drift; why Maria helps; what Sandro does.
