# AILEEN: one-page synopsis (workhorse draft)

In the mountain village of Tsnori, eighty people live without Elysium, the company that stores the dying as minds in a machine and has quietly drained the world of children. James, the village teacher, keeps telling them "one more year": one more year of greenhouses and they won't need Elysium's trucks, which come with a price, one villager every delivery.

But when the field officer Anton draws the priest's seven-year-old daughter Nino by lottery, James's wife Mira, secretly pregnant, volunteers in her place and leaves him a folded page. Therefore James hits Anton in the square, and Anton, who cannot take anyone in rage, burns the greenhouses and stops the deliveries. The village starves. A white star appears over the western ridge, and the priest confesses: Mira was carrying a child who should not exist. Therefore James, who can no longer feed anyone, walks to the Elysium gate to take her place.

But Mira is not there. She has vanished inside the system with a newborn, Aileen, who can open the Anchor and let everyone inside walk out. That would end Elysium, and the founder Lukas, who has held his wife Sissi back from dissolving into the shared Ocean for forty years, cannot allow it. Therefore Lukas offers James a deal: find them, and the supplies flow forever. Elysium cannot take a man by force, so James crosses of his own free will, the only way in.

But Mira is hiding in the Drift, an unmapped fringe of the Ocean that rearranges itself every few hours, and Elysium's tracking is useless there. Therefore Anton, already inside, needs James to read Mira's trail: her tags, her calligraphy, her habits. The two men chase her through a drowned city and a copy of a hospital ward. Aileen's forty-day anchoring is running, and forty days inside is only hours outside.

Therefore at the midpoint they find Mira, serene and almost merged, and Aileen, already a toddler. But Anton's orders are not to bring them home. They are to stop Aileen. Mira walks into the Ocean to let the child go, and James cannot stop her. Therefore he runs with Aileen, and Anton, who has followed him this far, must choose.

At the Anchor, Lukas makes James an offer: close the door and Mira returns, held in a boundary forever, as Sissi has been. Or open it and lose her for good. James remembers Mira's page. Therefore he opens the door and holds it with the one thing the system cannot force, his free yes. His body dies in the compound. Anton stands between James and Lukas's guards. Lukas walks through with Sissi.

Months later, the Village is rebuilding its greenhouses. A resonator salvaged from the ashes hums at night. A voice says, "Hey." Nino answers.
