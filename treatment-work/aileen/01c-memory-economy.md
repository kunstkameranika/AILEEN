# AILEEN: Act 2 high concept, combined (private)

**Pitch:** Orpheus in a descent where every door costs a memory, with the villain's man chasing him down. "Eternal Sunshine in reverse: he erases himself to reach her."

## Core rules (must stay simple)
1. **Every door asks a price in memory.** James chooses what to give, one memory per door.
2. **Price rises with depth.** Top layers take trivia (a smell, a song). Middle layers take skills and relationships. The last take identity.
3. **A spent memory is gone as FEELING, not as fact.** If he wrote it in his notebook or on a jar tag, he can read it, and feel nothing. (Reverse of Eternal Sunshine: he knows what he lost and cannot miss it.)
4. **Losing feels good.** Each payment lightens him. Calm creeps in. He starts to resemble Cy in the opening scene, the serenity he fears. Temptation to stop paying and stay.
5. **Anton hunts him and pays the same coin.** Anton's reasons for serving go first. By the depth where James is lost, Anton is too. (Judas arc.)
6. **Clock:** 40-day anchoring on Aileen, 40 days inside = hours outside. On day 40 she is freed or absorbed.

## Beats this economy forces
- **Midpoint:** he spends the unused-box memory. He is lighter and reaches Mira, and now doesn't understand why the child matters. She has to tell him. That is the reversal: Mira reminds him who he is.
- **Act 2b:** the page. He reads it and feels nothing. Then he reads what it says, which makes him choose again.
- **Crisis:** to hold the Anchor open for Aileen's door he must pay everything, including Mira, including himself. A true dilemma: keep the memory of his wife or let the others out.
- **Return:** the voice in the village resonator is the only part of him that is not memory.

## What the Hunt adds
Anton is the pursuer. The chase gives page-turn pace. The twist: the more Anton pays, the less he is the villain.
