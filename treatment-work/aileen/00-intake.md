## Contradictions / gaps
1. **Who is the father and who is the daughter?** James has no daughter in the script. Mira is pregnant when taken. Nino is Giorgi's. Lukas had a wife, no child stated.
2. **Cy vs Sissi.** The bible names Lukas's wife Sissi. The script's speaker is CY.
3. **Title AILEEN** is not a character in either file.
4. **Timeline.** Chamber scene is ~40 years before the village. Is it a prologue, or is Lukas somehow present now?
5. **The film's goal flips.** Script goal is "be worth a winter" (stop the embargo, find Mira). The old goal is "save the souls." Which comes first?
6. **Bible says Elysium never takes by force and consent is technical.** Yet the lottery and Anton's burn look coercive. The bible resolves it as mission, but the film must show it.
7. **Prior art.** What Dreams May Come (husband enters afterlife to retrieve wife) and Orpheus. Digital-afterlife: Upload, Pantheon, San Junipero, Soma, Altered Carbon, Transcendence. The specific hook is the Ocean (merging as pleasant erasure) plus mission-aid logic. Keep that.
