# AILEEN: Engine (private workhorse)

Writer decisions so far: Aileen is a GIFT. James returns in some form. A sacrifice is certain (Christ echo). Mira's choice, Lukas, Anton, climax meaning: open. Characters can be created and cut.

## Three engines (bake-off)

### A. "The Yes" (opponent: Anton and the consent rule)
- **Premise fuse:** Elysium cannot take anyone by force (bible 3). A coerced Quale does not anchor. So the villain's only weapon is making you want it. Starvation, mercy, a loved voice.
- **Opponent plan:** Anton needs a willing crossing. James hit him, so James is useless in the square ("Not you": a man in rage won't couple). Anton burns the greenhouses to remove everything keeping James in the Village. Grief and need do the rest. James walks in on his own.
- **Teeth:** fuel on the greenhouses (already in script).
- **Hook:** "The only film where heaven cannot take you by force, so the villain's weapon is how good it feels."
- **Climax idea:** James must give the one thing the system needs, a free yes, on his own terms, as the sacrifice.

### B. "The Cradle" (opponent: the Ocean itself)
- **Premise fuse:** The Ocean absorbs boundaries, gently. Aileen is the first Quale that keeps her edge and can touch the Ocean. The Ocean "wants" her the way the sea wants a river.
- **Opponent:** an impersonal pull, with Anton as its human face. Hard to dramatise: no plan, no scenes of opposition.
- **Hook:** "A father fights a paradise that never raises its voice."
- **Weakness:** fails the "passive opponent" test.

### C. "The Founder" (opponent: Lukas)
- **Premise fuse:** Lukas built it, lost Sissi to serenity, and has kept her from going for 40 years. He wants Aileen to keep the individual alive.
- **Opponent plan:** Lukas lures James, needs Aileen as the proof that boundaries can hold.
- **Hook:** "Orpheus meets the man who built the underworld."
- **Weakness:** makes Lukas a late, talky antagonist. Anton becomes redundant.

**Original recommendation (superseded): A, with C's Lukas as mentor-mirror.** A has the sharpest hook, an active opponent from the first scene, and it uses the writer's own script beats unchanged. Lukas stays the figure who failed at what James must do.

## Workhorse logline (A)
James, the failing leader of a mountain village that refuses Elysium's digital afterlife, enters Elysium to take his pregnant wife's place and win back the supplies that were cut. Elysium's field officer Anton cannot take anyone by force, so he needs James to say yes, and he has burned the village's last hope to get it. Inside, James must find Mira and the child she carries, and decide what a free yes is worth.

## [PROPOSALS] needing the writer
1. **Why "Not you":** Anton refuses James because rage will not couple. (Fits bible 3.)
2. **Aileen:** the first Quale that began in the Field with a mother (Mira, anchored pregnant), so she holds an edge and a connection at once. The star in the sky = her waking. She is a gift in a literal sense: proof that merging and self can coexist.
3. **Mira's choice (pick one):**
   a. She leaves to bear the child where it can live: out of love.
   b. Her dream told her to: out of faith (she's not a believer).
   c. She suspected the pregnancy would otherwise be taken: out of fear.
4. **Lukas (pick one):**
   a. Alive in the Chamber for 40 years, refusing to cross. Mentor-mirror.
   b. Anchored, still bounded, running on will. Late antagonist.
   c. Already in the Ocean; only the prologue shows him.
5. **James's sacrifice and return (pick one):**
   a. He gives his bounded Quale to hold the door open so Aileen can bring the others out. He returns as a voice in the village resonator: heard, not seen.
   b. He crosses willingly (the only yes), his body dies, he returns in Aileen: his edge is the part of her that stays.
   c. He trades his Quale for Mira's, who returns to bear the child on earth; he stays in the Ocean as a pattern the village can call.
6. **Anton:** keep as active opponent. His arc: believes sincerely, discovers the instruction comes from an Ocean that no longer wants anything. He may be the one who carries James's body home.

## CHOSEN ENGINE: "The Founder" (writer pick), revised

Writer picks: engine C. Aileen is a gift (proposal 2 accepted). Mira leaves out of LOVE. Lukas as proposed (see below). James's end: 4a.

### Fixes to C's two weaknesses
- **Lukas must act from page one.** Lukas runs Anton. The route to Tsnori, the lottery rhythm, the burned greenhouses are his plan. Lukas speaks in the Chamber scene (prologue), then reaches James through the resonator and Anton throughout.
- **Anton is not redundant.** He is Lukas's hands (opponent in the field) and later the Judas: the believer who learns the order came from a man, not the mission, and defects.

### Lukas [PROPOSAL, writer said "ok"]
Anchored, still bounded. For 40 years he has held his edge by will and kept Sissi (Cy) from dissolving by visiting her. Now she is almost gone. He cannot save her inside the Ocean. Then a child appears in the Field with an edge of her own (the star). Aileen is the first being who can hold a boundary while touching everyone.
- **Lukas wants:** Aileen, to raise a wall around the people he loves. Starting with Sissi.
- **Lukas cannot take her by force** (consent rule). Aileen answers to her family. So he needs James to walk in willingly and bring her to him.
- **His plan (adapts):** burn the Village's hope so James comes; offer him Mira's voice; offer the supplies; finally offer James the thing he wants most: not to lose his wife.
- **Teeth:** the greenhouses; the Village starves.

### Controlling idea (draft)
Love that holds on turns the beloved into a prisoner; love that opens the door lets them be free, at the cost of the one who opens it.

### Workhorse logline (50-100 words)
James, a village teacher who keeps promising his starving people "one more year" without Elysium's help, enters the digital afterlife to take the place of his pregnant wife and win back the supplies that were cut. Inside he finds the founder, Lukas, who has spent forty years refusing to let his own wife dissolve into the shared Ocean, and who needs James's miraculous daughter, Aileen, to build a wall around the people he loves. James must choose between holding on to Mira and Aileen, or opening the door for all of them.

### Hook
"The only film where the hero has to save the dead from the man who loves them too much."
