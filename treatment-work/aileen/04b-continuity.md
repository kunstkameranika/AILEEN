# AILEEN: Continuity (private)

Story days (no real calendar; weekdays are set by the church: Day 0 is a church/market Sunday).
| Day | Where | Event |
|---|---|---|
| 0 Sun | Tsnori | Prologue (40 yrs earlier, separate). Morning: kitchen. Church: lottery. Mira leaves. Greenhouses burn. |
| 1-6 | Tsnori | Starvation. |
| 7 night | Tsnori | Bell, white point (Aileen's birth inside). |
| 8 | Road | James walks (pre-dawn to late day). |
| 8 evening | Gate | James crosses. |
| 9 afternoon | Elysium | Aileen's 40-day clock ends. |

Ratio inside:outside = 1 hour outside per day inside (writer to confirm). Aileen's 40 days start at birth (Day 7 night). Clock ends about 40 hours later, Day 9 afternoon. James has about 20 inside days.

Map: Village on the ridge; mountain road down; abandoned farmhouse with the swing; Elysium dam and reservoir in the valley (a day's walk); gate in the dam.

Clocks: (1) the village's food, (2) Aileen's 40 days.
