# AILEEN: Meaning and ending (private)

All items are [PROPOSAL] unless the writer has fixed them (fixed: sacrifice, James returns in some form via a voice, Aileen is a gift).

## Values debate
- **Dominant (Lukas):** love is keeping. Hold the beloved, whatever it costs them.
- **Underdog (James):** love is opening. Let the beloved go, whatever it costs you.
- **Antagonist aria:** Lukas, on a screen or in the Chamber, to James: "I have talked to my wife every week for forty years. What have you done for yours?"
- **Mentor-substitute:** Mira's page and the Holdouts' old farmer.
- **Judas:** Anton, the believer who stays with Lukas, then defects.

## Antagonism ladder
Keeping (Lukas's love) -> contrary: Holding by force -> contradictory: Imprisoning -> negation of the negation: Preserving a person who is no longer there.

## Mira's page [PROPOSAL]
Folded twice, written in the dark in Act 1. Contents: "Don't follow. If you follow, don't bring us back. Open it." James reads it in the kitchen and follows anyway, for the supplies and for himself. The last line is what he must obey at the end.

## The Anchor door: what opening it does [PROPOSAL]
It lets each anchored person choose: stay in the Ocean, or leave as themselves. "Leave" means ending as individuals, in peace, back into the Field. Neither is called better. The film never says whether the Ocean is evolution or annihilation.

## Earned means
- **Tool:** James's free yes. Elysium cannot couple a forced Quale. The Anchor can only be held open by a willing one. Lukas and Anton cannot hold it. Only someone who consents, and who can still say "I let go", can.
- **Set up:** Act 1 burns the greenhouses to make James say yes (the yes is Lukas's own creation). Act 2 keeps him unwilling to say it until the last door.

## Crisis (true dilemma)
Lukas offers: close the door and Mira is brought back, held in a boundary, as Sissi has been, forever. Or open it, and Mira, Sissi and everyone walk into the Ocean or out of it, gone to him forever.

## Climax
James refuses Lukas, and chooses the open door. Aileen opens the Anchor. James stays at the threshold, giving his own edge to hold it. His body dies in the compound. Anton, who was sent to stop it, stands between James's body and Lukas's guards. Lukas steps through with Sissi.

## Decisive act
Embodies the underdog value. Costs James everything: Mira, his body, his return in person.

## Order of the three climaxes
External (the door opens, Elysium falls) -> Internal (James lets Mira go) -> Philosophical (the Ocean is neither saved nor condemned; the choice is the answer).

## Resolution / final image
The Village rebuilds the greenhouses. Giorgi and Nino salvage a resonator from the burned frames. Late at night it hums. A voice, altered and buzzy: "Hey." Nino answers. Cut to black. (Echo of the opening: "Hey. Lukas.")

## Controlling idea
Love that holds on turns the beloved into a prisoner. Love that opens the door lets them go free, at the cost of the one who opens it.

## Ending type
Up-ironic: the world is freed and James is gone, but not entirely.
