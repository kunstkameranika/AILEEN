# AILEEN: Act 2 high concept: "The Hunt" (private)

Writer picked option 3. Memory economy is shelved.

**Pitch:** A race to a child inside a world that rearranges itself every few hours. Two hunters, one who loves her, one who was sent to stop her.

## The place [PROPOSAL]
**The Drift.** An unmapped region at the edge of the Ocean where patterns that refuse to merge hide. It reshapes with the tide of the Ocean, every few hours inside, so Elysium cannot map it and tracking fails. The only way to find anyone is to follow a person's trail and know the person.
- Zones are made from the Ocean's leftovers: a drowned city, a copy of a hospital ward, a field of tags, a staircase that never ends. Each zone is a set piece.
- Residents: the Holdouts, anchored people who kept their edge and are hiding with Mira.

## Why James is not reactive
- **He knows Mira.** Her trail is made of her habits: jar tags, calligraphy, a doodle, how she folds a page. Anton and Elysium's tracking cannot read it. Each fork tests what only a husband knows.
- **He can lay false trails** for Anton using the same knowledge.
- **He decides** whom to trust, whom to leave, and when to run, at each zone.

## Why Anton is not the engine
- Anton arrives ahead of James with tracking that stops working in the Drift. He needs James's reading of Mira. Hunter and hunted are forced into an uneasy partnership (Midnight Run).
- Lukas pushes from outside. The clock is Aileen's 40 days: free or absorbed.
- The partnership cracks when James learns Anton's orders (stop her, not bring her back).

## Act 2 shape (proposal)
1. **Entering.** Lukas's deal, Maria's briefing, the gate. James crosses willingly (the only way).
2. **First tide.** Arrival in the Drift. Anton is already there. Tracking is dead. Mira's first tag.
3. **Trail.** Zone 1 and 2 (drowned city, ward). James reads Mira. Anton learns he needs him.
4. **Alliance.** The tide separates or threatens them. They survive together. Anton's cover: "I'm here to bring her home."
5. **Midpoint.** They find the Holdouts and Mira, who is serene. James meets Aileen, already a toddler. Reveal: Mira is almost merged, and Anton's orders are not to bring them back.
6. **Reversal.** Anton moves. Mira is taken or lost. James must run with Aileen. Hunter vs. hunted for real.
7. **Despair.** The Drift closes behind them. Day 39. The only way out is up through the Anchor.
8. **Climax setup.** Aileen opens the Anchor. James must hold it. Anton chooses.
